============================================================
F) Convergence tuning (for your current case)
============================================================
You observed:
- rounds_executed=10
- converged=false

This is expected when:
- max rounds is too small, or
- epsilon is too strict for your data scale.

Important: your features are not normalized and x goes up to 1,000,000.
So epsilon=0.001 is usually too strict.

Use this approach:
1) keep EARLY mode
2) increase rounds to 30 or 50
3) increase epsilon to 10, 50, or 100 (start with 50)

One-off run example (no config edit needed):
- hadoop jar /home/ds503/ds503_bdm-1.0-SNAPSHOT.jar project2.task2simple.KMeansMain /home/ds503/project2_task2_config.properties mode=EARLY rounds=50 epsilon=50 output.base=/project2/task2/output/early_r50_e50

Check result:
- hdfs dfs -cat /project2/task2/output/early_r50_e50/summary.txt
